About iPray Global

iPray Global is a God-inspired prayer movement uniting 1,000,000 youth across nations, denominations, and cultures. On December 12, 2025, believers from every continent will stand in 24-hour prayer chain— lifting one global cry to God for revival, salvation, nations, and the next generation.

OUR MISSION

To awaken a generation of youth in prayer, unity, fire, and burden for souls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The world is becoming darker—but the Light shines brighter. We are raising a generation that prays, intercedes, and stands for God’s Kingdom.
